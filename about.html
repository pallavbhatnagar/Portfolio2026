Beliefs I design by

  • I think in systems.

    Users, data, business rules and edge cases come first, so the design holds up in real use.

  • Make complexity readable.

    The right hierarchy shows what is happening and what to do next, without hiding the detail people need.

  • Tools change, judgement stays.

Who you'd be working with🤝

A curious designer who builds, and who values clarity over novelty.

The short version: I explore widely, question assumptions, test early and make sure every decision that ships has a reason behind it.

Built alongside engineers, PMs and researchers

Where I start

With the system, not the screen.

Before any screen, I learn who uses the product, what the data looks like and which rules and odd cases shape it. The design follows from that.

How I collaborate

I design in the open.

Rough flows and prototypes go up early, so engineers and product can shape them while they are still cheap to change.

After handoff

I stay until it ships.

I answer questions fast during the build and check the live version against the design before calling it done.

Teams usually bring me in to

  • Make dense data and complex workflows easy to read.
  • Design AI features people can check and trust.
  • Take an idea from a rough flow to a working prototype.

Questions people ask?

Short answers to what hiring teams usually ask me first.

What kind of role are you looking for?

A senior product design role on a team that builds complex products: B2B and enterprise software, data and AI tools, supply chain or fintech. I'm a UX designer today and ready for that step up.

I do my best work where design has a seat early, the team talks to its users, and success is measured after launch.

What have you owned end to end?

At Resilinc I take features from research to handoff: user interviews and testing, flows, interaction and visual design, and the prototype the team reviews.

I was also part of the small team that built a new design system on IBM's Carbon, and I've designed supply chain maps and AI-assisted workflows where people need to check the system's work.

Each case study on this site names my role and what I was responsible for.

How do you know a design worked?

We agree on what success looks like before designing: a task finished faster, fewer support questions, a decision made with confidence.

Then I test with real users before and after release. My first design job showed me why: the infographics I made at JECRC University grew active users by 13.5% in five months, and I've designed toward measurable outcomes since.

How do you work with product and engineering?

Early and in the open. Rough flows and prototypes go up while they're still cheap to change, so product and engineering shape them with me.

I write decisions down, document components and their states for handoff, and stay through the build to check the live version against the design.

Where are you based, and when can you start?

I'm based in New Delhi and open to remote, hybrid or on-site roles. I've worked with teams across India, Europe and the US, so splitting the day across time zones is routine.

I can start in about 30 days, and I'm happy to begin with a portfolio walkthrough or a short design exercise, whichever helps you decide.
